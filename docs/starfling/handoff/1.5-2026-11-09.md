### Slow card boots

Seen again: after K's power-on (11-06) boots 213-215 ran ~3x slow on SD
reads (menu 4.2-5.0 s), then normal. On 11-09 single slow boots also
appeared mid-round on warm reboots (242: menu 4.56 s; 250: game exec
1.63 s). So it is not only "after power-on". Rounds need n>=5 and a look
at outliers; medians are robust to one.
